## 1. Purpose
To state management's commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information and to establish the framework for the ISMS.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors and third parties accessing SecurePay information or systems within the ISMS scope (see `01-ISMS-Scope/ISMS-Scope.md`).

## 3. Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CEO / Management | Provide leadership and resources, approve the policy, review the ISMS at least annually |
| Security Lead | Operate the ISMS, report performance, maintain risk register |
| Asset / Risk Owners | Manage risks and controls for their assets |
| All personnel | Comply with policies and report incidents and weaknesses |

## 4. Requirements
1. Information security objectives are set annually and measured (e.g. MFA coverage, restore tests completed, training completion).
2. The ISMS is aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and continually improved.
3. Information is classified (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) and handled accordingly.
4. Risks are assessed and treated using the approved methodology.
5. Legal, regulatory and contractual obligations are identified and maintained, including personal data protection requirements.
6. All personnel receive security awareness training at onboarding and annually.
7. Policies are reviewed at least annually and after significant change.
8. Security incidents and suspected weaknesses must be reported immediately.

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-assessed, approved by the Security Lead (and the CEO for High risks), time-limited (maximum 6 months) and recorded in the exceptions log. Non-compliance may lead to disciplinary action under HR policy.
