## 1. Purpose
To define acceptable use of company systems, devices, internet, email and data.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors and third parties accessing HawkPay information or systems within the ISMS scope (see `01-ISMS-Scope/ISMS-Scope.md`).

## 3. Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All users | Use assets responsibly and report misuse |
| IT Manager | Enforce technical controls |
| Line managers | Ensure team awareness |

## 4. Requirements
1. Company systems are for business use; limited personal use must not create risk.
2. Do not install unapproved software or disable security tools.
3. Lock screens when unattended; keep a clear desk and clear screen.
4. Do not share credentials; use MFA.
5. Email and messaging: do not send Confidential/Restricted data externally without approval and protection; report phishing.
6. Do not copy customer data to personal devices or unapproved cloud storage.
7. Remote working must use the VPN and approved devices on trusted networks.
8. Activity on company systems may be monitored in line with law and HR policy.

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-assessed, approved by the Security Lead (and the CEO for High risks), time-limited (maximum 6 months) and recorded in the exceptions log. Non-compliance may lead to disciplinary action under HR policy.
