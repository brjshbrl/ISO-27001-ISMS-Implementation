## 1. Purpose
To identify, own, classify and protect information assets throughout their lifecycle.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors and third parties accessing HawkPay information or systems within the ISMS scope (see `01-ISMS-Scope/ISMS-Scope.md`). Applies to information, software, hardware, cloud and SaaS assets.

## 3. Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Asset Owner | Accountable for classification, protection and review of the asset |
| Custodian | Implements and operates controls |
| IT Manager | Maintains the asset inventory |
| Users | Return assets on exit and handle assets per classification |

## 4. Requirements
1. All assets are recorded in the Asset Inventory with owner, custodian, classification and C/I/A rating.
2. Classification levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted; handling rules apply to storage, transfer and disposal.
3. The inventory is reviewed at least annually and on significant change.
4. Assets are returned on termination or role change; access is revoked.
5. Media and devices are securely wiped before reuse or disposal and disposal is recorded.
6. Information labelling follows classification rules.
7. Laptops require full-disk encryption and endpoint protection.

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-assessed, approved by the Security Lead (and the CEO for High risks), time-limited (maximum 6 months) and recorded in the exceptions log. Non-compliance may lead to disciplinary action under HR policy.
