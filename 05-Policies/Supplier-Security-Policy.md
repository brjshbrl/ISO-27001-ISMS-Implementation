## 1. Purpose
To manage information security risks arising from suppliers and cloud services.

## 2. Scope
All suppliers with access to HawkPay information or systems, including AWS, Microsoft, GitHub, CRM, HR/payroll and support SaaS vendors.

## 3. Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Procurement | Run due diligence and maintain the supplier register |
| Security Lead | Assess security risk and set requirements |
| Legal/Finance | Include security and confidentiality clauses in contracts |
| Business owners | Monitor supplier performance |

## 4. Requirements
1. Suppliers are risk-tiered (Critical, High, Standard) based on data access and service dependency.
2. Due diligence before onboarding: security questionnaire and, for Critical suppliers, review of certifications/reports (e.g. ISO 27001, SOC 2).
3. Contracts include confidentiality, security requirements, incident notification and exit provisions.
4. Critical suppliers are reviewed annually; others every two years or on change.
5. Access granted to suppliers is least-privilege and removed at contract end.
6. Cloud services are used under the shared-responsibility model with documented configuration ownership.

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-assessed, approved by the Security Lead (and the CEO for High risks), time-limited (maximum 6 months) and recorded in the exceptions log. Non-compliance may lead to disciplinary action under HR policy.
