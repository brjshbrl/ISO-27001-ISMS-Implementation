## 1. Purpose
To ensure information security incidents are detected, handled and learned from in a consistent and timely way.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors and third parties accessing HawkPay information or systems within the ISMS scope (see `01-ISMS-Scope/ISMS-Scope.md`).

## 3. Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Security Lead | Incident coordinator; decides severity and escalation |
| IT / DevOps | Technical containment and recovery |
| Management | Approves external communication |
| All personnel | Report suspected incidents immediately |

## 4. Requirements
1. Phases: Detection -> Triage -> Containment -> Eradication -> Recovery -> Lessons learned.
2. Incidents are reported to the Security Lead as soon as detected; severity is assigned (Low/Medium/High/Critical).
3. Evidence is preserved and chain of custody maintained.
4. Customer and regulatory notification decisions are made by management with legal advice; timelines follow applicable obligations.
5. Every incident has a record and a post-incident review within 10 working days.
6. The plan is tested at least annually through a tabletop exercise.
7. Lessons learned feed the risk register and corrective actions.

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-assessed, approved by the Security Lead (and the CEO for High risks), time-limited (maximum 6 months) and recorded in the exceptions log. Non-compliance may lead to disciplinary action under HR policy.
